Funfetti Rainbow Cupcakes

Fluffy vanilla cupcakes loaded with colorful sprinkles and topped with creamy buttercream frosting!

12 Cupcakes

📋 Ingredients Checklist

  • 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1.5 tsp baking powder
  • 0.25 tsp salt
  • 0.5 cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • 0.75 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.5 cup whole milk
  • 0.5 cup rainbow sprinkles

👩‍🍳 Baking Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
  2. Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a medium mixing bowl.
  3. In a large bowl, beat the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then mix in the vanilla extract.
  5. Alternately add the dry mixture and milk into the batter, mixing until smooth.
  6. Gently fold in the rainbow sprinkles with a spatula.
  7. Divide batter evenly into muffin cups and bake for 18–20 minutes.
  8. Allow cupcakes to cool completely before topping with frosting and extra sprinkles!
